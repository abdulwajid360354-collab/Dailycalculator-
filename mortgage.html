The Science of Mortgage Planning

A mortgage is likely the largest financial liability you will manage. Our Mortgage Calculator is designed using international banking standards for amortization. By deconstructing your payment into Principal, Interest, Taxes, and Insurance (PITI), you gain a granular understanding of your equity growth. This precision is vital for long-term fiscal health.

Calculating Payments: The Formula

The core of this tool relies on the fixed-rate loan formula, ensuring mathematical sovereignty in your results:

Monthly Payment (M) = P [ i(1 + i)^n ] / [ (1 + i)^n – 1 ]

Where P represents the total loan principal, i is the monthly interest rate, and n is the number of months in the term. DailyCalculator automates this complex algebra to provide instant results.

How to Maximize Home Equity
